      Emergency red flags

      Know when urgent medical help may be needed

      This list is not exhaustive. If symptoms feel severe, sudden, or frightening, seek urgent medical help immediately.

      How to use this section: Scan these warning signs first. If any apply and symptoms feel severe, sudden, or rapidly worsening, choose urgent medical help over waiting for more online reassurance.

      Chest pain

      Seek urgent help if chest pain is severe, worsening, or paired with shortness of breath, fainting, or sudden weakness.

      Severe breathing difficulty

      Breathing difficulty may require urgent medical attention, especially if speech or movement becomes difficult.

      Fainting

      Sudden loss of consciousness should be treated seriously and may need urgent assessment.

      Confusion

      New confusion or disorientation can signal a more urgent problem.

      Severe allergic reaction

      Swelling, breathing problems, or rapidly worsening symptoms may require emergency care.

      Uncontrolled bleeding

      Bleeding that does not stop or feels severe should be treated as urgent.

      Sudden weakness or numbness

      Sudden one-sided weakness or numbness may require urgent evaluation.

      Severe dehydration

      Severe dehydration can become dangerous quickly, especially if dizziness or inability to keep fluids down is present.

      Urgent help guidance: If you are experiencing severe or life-threatening symptoms, seek urgent medical help immediately.

      Human consultation

      Why human medical review still matters

      CareGuide AI supports decision-making, but qualified healthcare professionals remain responsible for medical advice and treatment.

      A human clinician can ask follow-up questions, consider medical history, examine symptoms in context, and respond when symptoms change unexpectedly.

      • Seek help if symptoms persist or worsen.
      • Seek help if symptoms affect daily activities.
      • Seek help any time you are worried or uncertain.
      • Seek urgent help immediately if red-flag symptoms appear.

      Prototype consultation action

      This prototype does not book real appointments. In a real service, this action would connect users to qualified healthcare support.

      Privacy & AI limitations

      Keep the safeguards visible

      The safest design choice for this prototype is data minimisation and visible human oversight.

      This prototype does not store, upload, or share personal health information. Inputs are used only temporarily to simulate guidance on the page.

      The result is based on simple prototype logic and may not match real medical risk. Human verification is needed.

      CareGuide AI supports reflection and urgency awareness. It does not replace a qualified healthcare professional.

      The prototype avoids suggesting medication or treatment plans because those decisions require qualified medical judgement.
